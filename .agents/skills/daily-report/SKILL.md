# Trading-System Daily Report

1. Read `sessions/日报SOP.md` completely through the bounded `workflow_entry` transport defined in step 3; it is the committed executable authority for Codex runs. Never start with a direct raw read of the SOP.
2. If `.claude/skills/daily-report/SKILL.md` exists on this machine, treat it only as a local Claude Code compatibility mirror. It may be consulted for drift detection, but it is not allowed to override `sessions/日报SOP.md` or any committed authority file because `.claude/` is git-ignored.
3. Run `runtime/context_pack.py --date <date> --slot <slot>` once and inspect its `authority_sha256`, `authority_manifest`, `authority_read_plan`, and `read_policy.prelock_safe_read_policy`. Always read the mandatory guardrails in `read_policy.mandatory_guardrails_always_read`; expand the remaining authority files by the current phase/source need instead of loading the full authority set blindly. The skill itself is loaded exactly once by the skill entrypoint and remains hash-tracked in the authority manifest; `workflow_entry` therefore contains the full SOP only and must not reload this skill. Read `workflow_entry` and every later phase through the bounded transport protocol: run `runtime/phase_contract.py ... --phase <phase> --emit-part 1`, inspect `part_count` and `bundle_sha256`, then read every remaining part index exactly once. Every part must have the expected BEGIN/END marker, the same bundle hash and part count, continuous one-based indexes, and `part_bytes<=28672`; a missing, duplicate, out-of-order, oversized, hash-mismatched, or truncated part is a hard stop. Do not directly reread the persisted bundle or its source files. Analysis authority remains split into `analysis_orderflow`, `analysis_chart_winrate`, and `analysis_agents`; all three are mandatory and together equal the former full analysis set. The logical phase bundle must report `complete=true` and remain within its logical byte limit; transport splitting never permits omitting or shortening a method file. If the authority digest changed unexpectedly or the phase need is ambiguous, hard-stop for maintenance instead of improvising an untracked read. Before the analysis lock, never directly read the full context ledger, aggregate memory, prior reports, old outputs, old runtime state, reviews, quarantine files, or prior analysis/decision archives. Do not run naked repository-wide search for pre-lock facts; use explicit paths from the context pack/read plan or the safe rg excludes from `runtime/prelock_read_policy.py`.
4. Preserve the Claude Code-era guardrails from committed authority, not from the ignored `.claude` mirror. These are mandatory: `memory/independent-report-no-user-reference.md`, `memory/precise-numbers-no-fuzzy.md`, `memory/report-chinese-only-narrative.md`, `memory/notion-publish-checklist.md`, and `memory/stability-contract.md`.
   `analysis_agents` is a compatibility phase name for the current reasoning-depth and scoring contracts. The historical `agents/` and `state/` design layer must never appear in the production authority manifest or any phase bundle.
5. Keep this workflow isolated. Do not invoke or mix any sibling or cross-workflow process.
   Daily eligibility has exactly one pre-report and pre-trade gate: the same-day Notion `开仓前状态门禁` row and its final `主观结论`. `心智自控日记表` and `心力监控模块` are end-of-day review inputs only; never query, require, infer from, or use them to modify report/trading eligibility. A missing, wrong-date, or internally contradictory gate row is a hard stop for manual confirmation, not permission to substitute review data.
   The morning slot must persist that one live read as `runtime_state/<date>/pretrade_gate.json` by running `session_runner.py record-pretrade-gate` under the active morning lease. The command records the Notion page id, observation time, five gate fields and final conclusion, validates contradictions, and writes an immutable hash-bound contract. Midday and evening must reuse this exact same-day contract and must never re-query Notion, read a prior report, or accept a prompt/automation-memory summary as gate authority. `prepare` mechanically binds the contract into the slot transition; missing, stale, changed or wrong-date contracts hard-stop.
6. Optimize only deterministic collection and mechanical execution.
7. Before the analysis lock, do not read or use Notion actual-position facts for Maker/main decisions. Positions may be reconciled only after the independent report hash is locked, and may not change score, win rate, direction, entry, stops, targets, or whether Maker places an order.
8. Before collection, run `session_runner.py begin-session --date <date> --slot <slot>` and retain its lease token. Pass that token to capture and every subsequent runner command. A second slot/date may not begin until delivery closes the lease; interruption requires explicit `abandon-session` or `recover-session` with a reason. If an immutable same-date/same-slot capture manifest already completed successfully before an audited hard stop closed the original lease, do not edit the manifest and do not hand-copy evidence. Resume only by running `session_runner.py recover-session --date <date> --slot <slot> --from-lease-id <capturing_lease_id> --reason <explicit reason>` and then `prepare` with the new lease token and the exact old manifest path; the runner must record `recovered_from_workflow_lease` and `capture_lease_recovery`. If the archived lease cannot be validated, rerun capture instead of bypassing the lease gate.
   For morning only, immediately after `begin-session` and before capture, read the one same-day Notion gate row and run `session_runner.py record-pretrade-gate --date <date> --slot morning --source-page-id <page_id> --source-observed-at <ISO> --subjective-conclusion <可交易|不可交易> --emotion-stable <true|false> --rhythm-consistent <true|false> --result-dependency <true|false> --continuous-stop-loss-count <n> --physical-condition <normal|tired|unwell> --lease-token <token>`. Midday/evening do not run this command; their `prepare` only validates and binds the morning artifact.
   Report Gate/Lease creation is mutually exclusive with `runtime/authority_mutex.py` maintenance windows. If an authorized maintainer holds that mutex, hard-stop before production; never delete or bypass the maintenance lock. Maintainers must claim the mutex before changing authority/runtime files and release it in a `finally`-equivalent cleanup after validation. If a maintainer hard-stops and loses the token, recovery is two-stage: after rechecking no active Gate/Lease and obtaining explicit authorization, run `authority_mutex.py request-recovery --reason <explicit orphan evidence>`; preserve its challenge and exact lock SHA, wait the enforced five-minute cooldown, recheck that the lock is still orphaned and unchanged, then run `authority_mutex.py recover --challenge <challenge> --expected-lock-sha256 <sha> --confirm-orphaned`. Direct deletion and immediate recovery remain forbidden.
9. Capture into the immutable manifest path printed by `capture_all.js`/`capture_delta.js`. A full baseline run must use `capture_all.js`, which runs `cleanup.js` first; do not bypass it with manual sub-captures. For midday/evening, `capture_delta.js` is still required to refresh all six sources; delta means conservative sequencing, source fingerprint comparison, and one bounded full fallback, not permission to skip FedWatch or reuse morning/prior evidence. Capture cleanup only removes expired dynamic inbox screenshots and BlockBeats cards older than `CLEANUP_KEEP_DAYS` days, never current-day evidence or immutable snapshots. Post-delivery retention cleanup is automatic: successful `session_runner.py deliver` executes `runtime/report_retention.py`, writes `runtime_state/<date>/retention_cleanup_<slot>.json`, keeps canonical local Word drafts, Notion pages, delivery/lease/automation minimal state, and removes older reproducible local non-Word artifacts according to `REPORT_RETENTION_KEEP_DAYS` days. Use `--skip-retention-cleanup` only for explicit troubleshooting. BlockBeats must be collected with the page's `重要快讯` filter selected and must cover all available important flashes from the 12 hours before the capture time; require `blockbeats.json` to contain `"important_only": true`, `"capture_window_hours": 12`, `"window_covered": true`, at least three `candidate_rows`, exactly three `selected_rows`, and `rows` equal to the selected top3 by fundamental-impact priority. Farside BTC ETF Flow is a required source: require `farside_btc_etf.png` and `farside_btc_etf.json`, with title `Bitcoin ETF Flow (US$m)`, a parsed Total column, and numeric latest daily `total_usd_m`. Hard-stop instead of falling back to unfiltered news, a shorter BlockBeats window, stale ETF-flow data, or inferred ETF-flow numbers. After collection and before source validation, run `runtime/host_capability.py --date <date> --slot <slot> --thread-id $env:CODEX_THREAD_ID --minimum-effort high`; only its verified session-JSONL model/effort manifest can authorize `session_runner.py prepare --host-capability-manifest <exact path>`. Prompt text or the legacy `--max-reasoning-confirmed` flag alone is never host-effort evidence.
   `prepare` also activates `runtime/prelock_guard.py`: prior-session judgment artifacts (`sessions/context_log.md`, prior `analysis_*.json`/`decision_*.json`, prior combined/payload/render outputs) are quarantined until `lock-analysis` succeeds, and the guard manifest embeds the safe pre-lock read policy. The repository `.rgignore` hides known prior-judgment surfaces from default `rg`, but this is a guardrail, not permission to search broadly. Do not bypass this guard, manually restore/read quarantine files, or search `sessions/archive/**`, old `outputs/**`, old `runtime_state/**`, `reviews/**`, or `memory/MEMORY.md` before the report hash is locked. `lock-analysis` restores guarded files for post-lock continuity review; `abandon-session`/`recover-session` restore them on interruption.
10. Use `gpt-5.6-sol` with `high` reasoning effort for automations and preserve full workflow depth for source validation, visual transcription, analysis, scoring, win rate, risk, scenarios, decisions, writing, and review. `max` is compatible but not required.

11. Recompute the complete analysis from the report slot's freshly collected full valid six-source evidence for every morning, midday, and evening report. Never reuse prior evidence snapshots, features, scores, win rate, risk, or judgment. The six required sources are BlockBeats, Coinglass, Exocharts, FedWatch, Farside BTC ETF Flow, and TradingView. Farside must be used as the spot-side liquidity confirmation signal: price down + ETF outflow confirms/strengthens bearish health, price up + ETF inflow confirms/strengthens bullish health, and disagreement weakens the move. A logic/methodology hash change invalidates analysis only and never authorizes redundant evidence capture.
    Golden reasoning alignment is the learned compact contract in `scoring/golden_report_reasoning_alignment.md`, not a runtime comparison against historical reports. After `prepare`, the runner writes `runtime_state/<date>/golden_reasoning_<slot>.json`; do not reopen the 2026-06-22..26 AI Agent Word/Notion samples or manual Notion reports during production. The AI samples contribute logic chain, trading methodology and depth only; manual samples contribute user-intent/field semantics only; neither contributes old facts, direction, scores, conviction, prices or wording. VRVP, liquidity hunt, liquidation map and ETF flow are additive only and may not replace the original method chain. Do not pad the MD after output to mimic length, price-reference count, or rubric count.

Freeze that contract and run `runtime/adversarial_review.py`; besides the five check types it must provide evidence-bound `claim_verdicts` for every high-impact claim, citing concrete support/counter/resolution nodes. Run `prepare-report-contract` and copy its three canonical lines exactly once. From 2026-07-12 midday onward, any missing contract, generic unbound review, projection loss, representative entry, fake probability or fake MAX claim is a hard stop.
